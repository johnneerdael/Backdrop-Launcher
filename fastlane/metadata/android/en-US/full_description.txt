Backdrop Launcher gives your Android TV a cinematic home built around your apps and your artwork.

• Create, arrange and hide app rows.
• Resume Continue Watching items shared by compatible TV apps.
• Enjoy included offline backdrops or connect Trakt for movie and show artwork.
• Choose app artwork, accent colors, focus outlines and tile background opacity.
• Customize the clock, date and quiet clock screensaver.
• Restore locally cached home artwork on startup with no added branding splash.

Trakt provides backdrop selections; it does not synchronize viewing history. Continue Watching availability depends on your TV firmware and the apps that publish resume items.

Open source, GPL v3 or later. Built on Arc Launcher, LTvLauncher and FLauncher.
