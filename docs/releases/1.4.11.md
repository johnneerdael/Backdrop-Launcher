- Add TV channels under Settings → Applications. Choose which channels your apps publish, and each one becomes a row on Home that you can move, rename or hide like any other row.
- Prefer posters over still images on channel rows. A still image is used when an app has no poster or its poster cannot be loaded.
- Select a program to open it in its app. Hold Select to open the row's settings.
- Keep channel rows and their posters on the TV, so Home shows them straight away after a restart.
