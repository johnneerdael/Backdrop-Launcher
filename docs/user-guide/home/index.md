# Your home

Home brings together a backdrop, your app rows, an optional Continue Watching area and a status bar. The selected row is prominent; a faint preview below it lets you see that another row is available.

![A real home screen with Continue Watching, app cards and the next row peeking below](../assets/screenshots/home.webp){ width="1280" height="720" loading="lazy" }

## Open something

Move Left/Right to an app and press **Select** to launch it. Press Down for the next row or Up to return; the row remembers the focused app. A long row scrolls horizontally as you move to later cards.

If **Continue Watching** is visible, press Up from the first app row. Left/Right browses its programmes and Select hands the chosen item to the app that supplied it. [Learn about compatibility and filters](continue-watching.md).

## Put your apps in order

Rows appear on Home when they are enabled, contain at least one app and have a visible member. You can put an app in more than one row. For example, the same music app can appear in both “Daily” and “Music”.

- [Create, sort or hide a row](rows.md).
- [Move a card, hide an app or change its artwork](apps.md).
- [Show row titles or app names below cards](../personalise/appearance.md#labels-sound-and-focus-feedback).

## If Home has no apps

The **Add apps to a row in Settings** control leads focus to the Settings gear. Open it, choose **Applications**, then add a row or edit an existing one. Check that the row is shown, has checked app members and that those apps are not globally hidden.

## Set the scene

[Backdrops](../personalise/backdrops.md) provide the artwork behind your rows. Continue Watching’s **Immersive mode** can use the selected programme’s image instead. Choose the view that suits your Home, then [adjust colours and card backgrounds](../personalise/appearance.md) for readability.
